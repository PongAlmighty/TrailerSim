    The math

    Axle-scale worksheet

    Weigh each axle, measure its distance from the reference datum line (RDL), and the centre of balance is Σ(D × W) ÷ gross weight. Any consistent units work.

    Method from the Engineers Edge trailer weight and balance page. Vehicle and trailer presets are representative figures for each class, not manufacturer specifications: use the ratings on your door-jamb sticker and trailer VIN plate. The sway animation and onset speed are an illustration of the trend, not a prediction.
